# Sadhanapada App

A Progressive Web Application (PWA) designed for sadhakas to manage daily Sadhana tracking, practice reference guidance, guided audio sessions, personal journaling, and essential ashram information — 100% private and offline-capable.

## Features

- **Practices Guidance**: Step-by-step process sequences and guidelines for:
  - Yogasana (31 postures)
  - Angamardhana (31 processes)
  - Shakti Chalana (8 kriyas & Shoonya)
  - Surya Kriya & Surya Shakti
- **Guided Yogasanas**: Audio practice session with background playback support.
- **Daily Reflection & Tracker**: Custom reflection parameters, daily/weekly habit tracking, and progress reviews.
- **Personal Notes**: Date-wise private journal and reflection diary.
- **Settings & Astronomy**:
  - Independent toggles for Lunar Day (Tithi) and Sunrise/Sunset times.
  - Automatic internet IP location lookup with zero location permission prompts.
  - Backup & restore options for journal and notes.
- **Extras**:
  - **Yogic Diet**: Comprehensive daily meal guidance, foods to add/avoid, and therapeutic lifestyle rules.
  - **Contacts & Timings**: Verified Ashram contact directory and bus schedules (Gandhipuram ↔ Isha Yoga Center, Bengaluru).

## How to Install (Chrome PWA)

1. Open the app link in Google Chrome on your device.
2. Tap the Chrome menu icon (**⋮**) in the top right corner.
3. Select **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
4. Confirm by tapping **Install**.

The app is now added to your device home screen and works offline anytime!
